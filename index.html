Please complete this at least 24 hours in advance if you plan to attend the optional Saturday Review Session via Google Meet.

These sessions are optional and meant to support you as you move through the course at your own pace.

Students who sign up for a session and fail to attend without updating their response or notifying an instructor will be required to register for review sessions on a week-to-week basis moving forward.

CPR Skills Check-Off Sign-Up

CPR certification is a two-step process: you must complete the American Heart Association online training (Part 1) prior to attending your in-person skills check-off (Part 2). Once both of these steps are completed, you'll then obtain your CPR certification.

Course: BLS Provider CPR Online
Cost: $37 (paid directly to the AHA)

You must bring your completion certificate (printed or digital) to your check-off appointment.

Choose the Day That Fits Your Timeline We host two in-person skills check-off days. Pick an early day if you want to start ride-alongs sooner, or Academy Week if you don't need certification before then.
You can sign up or change your day any time up until 30 days before your chosen check-off day. After that, this locks — email us if you need to make a change.
