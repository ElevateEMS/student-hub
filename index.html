📚 Saturday Review Session Sign-Up
Please complete this at least 24 hours in advance if you plan to attend the optional Saturday Review Session via Google Meet.

Signing up helps us know whether to hold the session that week. If no students are signed up by Friday morning, the session will be canceled. We review responses each week to determine whether the session will be held.

These sessions are optional and meant to support you as you move through the course at your own pace.

Note: If you sign up for a review session and later become unavailable, you must update your response or notify an instructor as soon as possible. Instructors will wait up to 15 minutes for scheduled attendees before canceling the session. Students who sign up for a session and fail to attend without updating their response or notifying an instructor will be required to register for review sessions on a week-to-week basis moving forward.

CPR Skills Check-Off Sign-Up

A valid BLS CPR certification is required in order to complete this program and become an EMT. Certification requires passing an in-person skills check-off — we offer two ways to do that, and what you need to prepare depends on which one you pick below.

If you're only scheduling the Skills Check-Off at Academy Week, the training below is required to be completed beforehand:

Course: BLS Provider CPR Online
Cost: $37 (paid directly to the AHA)

You must bring your completion certificate (printed or digital) to your check-off appointment.

Choose the Day That Fits Your Timeline Pick the earlier full CPR course if you plan to schedule your ride-alongs through Elevate EMS — you must have your CPR certification before scheduling ride-alongs with our clinical sites. Or check off at Academy Week if you don't need certification before then. Either option is open to any student.
You can sign up or change your day any time up until 30 days before your chosen check-off day. After that, this locks — email us if you need to make a change.

Attendance & Cancellation Policy No call/no-shows are not acceptable. If you need to cancel, significant advance notice is required so that date can be given to another student.
